## 3. What the author claims

Read from the upstream README at the pinned commit (sha256 `92dd726a7c9a1e2859da423612a82c4d91524a2c4722dc7c904026c324e39e72`), before any result was produced here:

- Stated conditions: BTC/USDT spot, 1h candles, 0.1% fee per side, no leverage, single position, 8 years of data.
- This strategy (EmaCross, the core without the funding rule): total return 1353%, max drawdown -49.4%.
- Selection: picked from a 42-strategy benchmark in which 33 lost money outright.
- The author's own stated weaknesses:
  - regime concentration: One window, H2 2020 at +182%, carries a lot of this. Dropping it leaves +335% against BTC's +734% - i.e. the remainder loses to buy-and-hold.
  - selection effect: The strategy was picked as the winner of a 42-way benchmark and then had a fourth rule added; upstream names both selection steps itself.
  - benchmark fairness: Upstream states the benchmark is unfair to multi-pair strategies.
  - backtest only: Upstream states explicitly that this is backtest only, not live-tested.
